003 // Primary master mark // Construction diagram

Construction diagram

The master mark is the lowercase neo-grotesque wordmark tmrw, an athletic forward slash raked at an exact 23.0 degrees, and a baseline coordinate dot. The slash and dot are Kinetic Volt. Never redraw the slash by eye.

Athletic slash rake is locked at 23.0 degrees from vertical. Exclusion is a uniform 0.4em box measured from the live mark, never from the artboard.

004 // Brand positioning and strategic mission04 / 27

004 // Positioning

Sovereign sports commercialisation

tmrw/. is sovereign sports commercialisation infrastructure for Australia and New Zealand. Enterprise capital is routed to hometown athletes who already command suburban culture, then settled on a direct Stripe Connect rail disclosed as a 70/30 split. The unit of value is the postcode, not the national broadcast jersey.

Fifteen-kilometre catchment

Allocator value is measured inside a 15 km suburban radius of a stated storefront or club ground. Rings at 5 km, 10 km, and 15 km are the only permitted spatial diagram.

Automated statutory clearance

AusTriathlon commercial clearance and equivalent amateur eligibility gates must confirm before settlement can execute. Name, image, and likeness licences stay scoped to catchment.

Direct Connect rails

Athletes receive 70 percent of the collaboration purse. Platform and partner overhead occupy the remaining 30 percent. The ratio is public telemetry, never a hidden retainer.

  • Prioritising Saturday foot traffic over passive broadcast airtime.
  • Standardising fleet deployment so fifty verified athletes can be live before kickoff.
  • Refusing club emblems, team logos, and trademarked jerseys in generated media to protect NRL and Shute Shield compliance.

008 // Prohibited anti-patterns08 / 27

008 // Do not ship

Treatments that void the mark

  • Do not distort, stretch, outline, or extrude the wordmark paths.
  • Do not alter the 23.0 degree rake of the athletic slash.
  • Do not introduce blue, cobalt, cyan, or any cool substitute for Kinetic Volt.
  • Do not use em-dashes in headlines, body copy, legal clauses, or interface strings.
  • Do not place team logos, club emblems, or trademarked jerseys in generated film or stills.
  • Do not drop a shadow, gradient, or glow onto the master mark itself.
  • Do not set the wordmark in all caps, title case, or a second type family.
  • Do not insert the letter t into the micro-mark or favicon.
  • Do not recode Structural Hairlines as 2px rules or solid white strokes.
  • Do not replace the 5 / 10 / 15 km radar with a single gestural circle.
009 // Clearspace and exclusion boundaries09 / 27

009 // Clearspace

Strict 0.4em exclusion

Clearspace is a uniform 0.4em box measured from the outermost live ink of the wordmark, slash, and coordinate dot. Em is taken from the rendered wordmark size, never from the artboard.

  • Keep photography, rules, and partner marks outside the red boundary.
  • Do not inset the mark into a coloured tile that clips the 0.4em field.
  • When the mark sits in a crop, retain the full exclusion and letterbox the crop instead.
  • Sub-brand descriptors in section 013 sit outside this box, never inside it.
010 // Descriptor usage10 / 27

010 // Line up your future

Descriptor construction

The descriptor is always uppercase, always JetBrains Mono, always tracked to 0.35em. It sits below the mark, never beside it, and never in Inter.

Desktop

Wordmark at 24pt. Descriptor at 8px. Hold 8px between the coordinate-dot baseline and the descriptor cap height.

Mobile

Wordmark at 18pt. Descriptor at 7px. Do not translate, localise, or sentence-case the line LINE UP YOUR FUTURE.

011 // Micro-mark and favicon system11 / 27

011 // Isolated slash and coordinate

Sixteen-pixel squircle

Omission rule

The micro-mark is solely the athletic slash and the baseline coordinate dot. The letter t is strictly omitted at every favicon, app icon, and NFC face.

  • Draw on a 16px grid inside a rounded squircle with a 22 percent corner radius.
  • Keep the slash at 23.0 degrees even when the canvas is 16px.
  • Seat the dot on the same baseline row as the slash foot.
  • Export master at 1024px, then downsample. Do not redraw at 16px by hand.

015 // Typesetting governance and editorial standards15 / 27

015 // Voice

New Zealand English. Zero em-dashes.

Copy is written in New Zealand English for both Australian and New Zealand markets. The voice is institutional, direct, and suburban. It does not perform agency swagger.

  • Use commercialisation, standardising, prioritising, organisation, licence, colour, and centre.
  • Do not use em-dashes anywhere in product copy, legal text, captions, or this specification.
  • Separate clauses with periods, colons, or a double slash in taxonomy eyebrows.
  • Write bullet lists as complete sentences. Do not bold a lead-in term before the explanation.

Taxonomy eyebrows

Manifesto // Ground truth 01

Eyebrows are uppercase JetBrains Mono. The double slash is the only permitted section divider. Keep the wordmark lowercase as tmrw/. when it appears inside a sentence.

016 // Dynamic design system A16 / 27

016 // Framing beam

The 23.0 degree structural rule

System A is a single 1px Kinetic Volt beam locked at 23.0 degrees. It is a layout instrument, not decoration. Use it to crop photography, hold a folio, or split a 16:9 field.

  • Never rotate the beam to a friendlier angle for a single composition.
  • Never thicken the beam beyond 1px on screen or 0.25pt in print.
  • The beam may clip imagery. It may not clip the master mark or its 0.4em exclusion.
017 // Dynamic design system B17 / 27

017 // Modular cadence

Beam intersections become snap points

Cards, captions, and telemetry chips align to the nearest intersection of the 12-column institutional grid and the 23.0 degree beam.

018 // Dynamic design system C18 / 27

018 // WebGL line constraints

Atmospheric contours only

System C permits a family of faint lines running parallel to the 23.0 degree beam behind hero fields. These lines are atmospheric. They must not compete with the master mark.

  • Hold companion lines at 8 to 12 percent Signal White. Never colour them Kinetic Volt except for the single primary beam.
  • Keep line weight at 1px. Do not animate thickness.
  • This section constrains graphic atmosphere only. It does not authorise edits to the production Three.js hero canvas or its shaders.

024 // Physical packaging and athlete kits24 / 27

024 // Kit tagging

Monochrome field marks

Athlete kit labels

Woven or heat-transfer labels reproduce the standalone mark in black on undyed cotton, or white on black tape. No Kinetic Volt on on-field kit. No club crests appear beside the mark.

Packaging

Shipper cartons are unbleached kraft with a one-colour black stamp of the micro-mark. Inner tissue is #FBFBF9. Hangtags use the descriptor lockup at or above 45 mm.

025 // Hardware badges and NFC tokens25 / 27

025 // Tokens

Eighteen-millimetre faces

Hardware tokens carry the micro-mark only. The letter t is omitted. Faces are 18 mm at the shortest edge, anodised black aluminium, Kinetic Volt fill in the machined slash and dot.

  • NFC payloads resolve to a catchment-scoped athlete or allocator record. They never deep-link to a club property.
  • Laser-etch the folio 025 and a six-character batch code on the reverse in 5pt mono.
  • Do not print URLs on the face. Do not add enamel colour beyond Kinetic Volt.
