# Daily social campaigns | 19 November 2026

**Launch goal:** Introduce tmrw/. to people who have never heard of us. Start from zero followers: give each viewer a reason to follow, share or nominate someone. No athlete names, likenesses or confirmed athlete postcodes appear in this launch.

**Visual direction for all three:** Raw, high-contrast monochrome. Use `#080808` for backgrounds and `#F7F7F6` for text. Reserve volt green `#B8FF00` for the period in `tmrw/.` Use original footage, plain unbranded clothing and blank props. Keep team logos, club emblems and trademarked jerseys out of every frame.

## Instagram Reel | It Starts on Your Court

**Format:** 14-second vertical Reel. Film at anonymous local courts and fields. Frame tightly so no signs, landmarks, faces or identifying marks are visible. Leave the location tag off.

| Time | Picture and sound | On-screen copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 2 s | A ball hits a painted court line. Let the bounce break the silence. | `EVERY GAME STARTS SOMEWHERE.` |
| 2 to 7 s | Cut from the white line on a court to a white line on a field. Keep the squeak of the ball and the rustle of grass. | `YOUR COURT. YOUR FIELD. YOUR POSTCODE.` |
| 7 to 11 s | Floodlights click on, tightly cropped against black. | `MEET tmrw/.` |
| 11 to 14 s | End on black, then loop back to the opening bounce. | `FOLLOW FOR LOCAL SPORT.` |

**Caption:** First post. We’re tmrw/. We’re here to help local businesses back athletes and grassroots clubs. Follow to see how it works, and send this to the mate who always gets the game going.

**Measure:** Shares and follows from the Reel.

## TikTok | Clock In. Run On.

**Format:** 24-second vertical video. Cast an unnamed actor in plain, unbranded work clothes and match kit. Keep `DRAMATISATION` legible throughout. The shift and match are fictional. Film the match side in the style of a state league broadcast, using original footage and no broadcaster graphics.

| Time | Picture and sound | On-screen copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 3 s | An alarm reads 6:00. Cut from the buzz to a whistle in an empty tunnel. | `6 AM CASUAL SHIFT / MATCHDAY` |
| 3 to 10 s | Match cuts: lace work boots, lace footy boots; pull on a work glove, tape a wrist. | `SAME HANDS. DIFFERENT SHIFT.` |
| 10 to 18 s | A clock-in beep gives way to a wide shot of the actor running onto the field. | `YOU SEE KICK-OFF. NOT THE SHIFT BEFORE IT.` |
| 18 to 24 s | Cut to black with `tmrw/.` and hold the final line long enough to read. | Nominate the athlete in your postcode |

**Caption:** Dramatisation. You see the game. You don’t always see the shift that came first. Follow tmrw/. and share this with the mate who goes straight from work to training. To nominate someone, send us a private message with your postcode. Ask the athlete before sharing their details.

**Measure:** Full watches, shares and private messages about nominations.

## LinkedIn | The A$2,500 Receipt

**Format:** Four-slide portrait document post. Put `ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE / NOT A PAYMENT` on every slide. Use type only, with the same type size for each share.

| Slide | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | `IF A LOCAL BUSINESS PUT IN A$2,500, WHO WOULD GET WHAT?` |
| 2 | `A$1,750 / ATHLETE (70%)` |
| 3 | `A$375 / GRASSROOTS CLUB (15%)` |
| 4 | `A$375 / TMRW (15%)` and `SEE THE WHOLE SPLIT BEFORE YOU BACK LOCAL SPORT.` |

**Post copy:** We’re tmrw/. Here’s how a local business could support an athlete and a grassroots club in one deal.

In this sample, A$2,500 is split three ways: A$1,750 goes to the athlete, A$375 goes to the grassroots club and A$375 goes to TMRW to run the sponsorship. It’s an example, not a payment or a promise of earnings.

What would A$375 help your local club pay for first: junior entries, gear for new starters or something else? Share this with a local business that backs sport, and follow for more examples.

**Measure:** Reposts and replies naming a specific use for the club share.
