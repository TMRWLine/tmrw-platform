# TMRWLine launch campaigns | 19 November 2026

**Launch goal:** Introduce TMRWLine, styled `TMRW/.`, and its tagline, `Line up your Future`. Start from zero followers: give each viewer a reason to follow, share or nominate someone. No athlete names, likenesses or confirmed athlete postcodes appear in this launch.

**Visual direction for all three:** Raw, high-contrast monochrome. Use `#080808` for cover and end-card backgrounds and `#F7F7F6` for text. Reserve volt green `#B8FF00` for the period in `TMRW/.` Use original footage, plain unbranded clothing and blank props. Keep team logos, club emblems and trademarked jerseys out of every frame.

## Instagram Reel | It Starts on Your Court

**Format:** 14-second vertical Reel. Film at anonymous local courts and fields. Frame tightly so no signs, landmarks, faces or identifying marks are visible. Leave the location tag off.

**Cover:** Use the [court graphic](../launch/assets/instagram-court-cover.png) with `IT STARTS ON YOUR COURT` in off-white and `TMRW/.` below.

| Time | Picture and sound | On-screen copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 2 s | A ball hits a painted court line. Let the bounce break the silence. | `EVERY GAME STARTS SOMEWHERE.` |
| 2 to 7 s | Cut from the white line on a court to a white line on a field. Keep the squeak of the ball and the rustle of grass. | `YOUR COURT. YOUR FIELD. YOUR POSTCODE.` |
| 7 to 11 s | Floodlights click on, tightly cropped against black. | `MEET TMRW/.` |
| 11 to 14 s | End card: centre the logo and tagline on black with the follow line beneath, then loop back to the opening bounce. | `TMRW/.`, `Line up your Future`, `FOLLOW FOR LOCAL SPORT.` |

**Music brief:** Spare, percussive instrumental, 95 to 105 BPM. Search `minimal percussion`, `low pulse` or `sports build` in Instagram’s in-app commercial-use music library. Keep the first bounce dry and the beat under the court sounds. If no cleared track is available, use the original court sounds alone.

**Caption:** First post. We’re TMRWLine. We’re here to help local businesses back athletes and grassroots clubs. Follow to see how it works, and send this to the mate who always gets the game going.

Line up your Future

**Hashtags:** #TMRWLine #GrassrootsSport #LocalSport

**@ tags:** None.

**Measure:** Shares and follows from the Reel.

## TikTok | Clock In. Run On.

**Format:** 24-second vertical video. Cast an unnamed actor in plain, unbranded work clothes and match kit. Keep `DRAMATISATION` legible throughout. The shift and match are fictional. Film the match side in the style of a state league broadcast, using original footage and no broadcaster graphics.

**Cover:** Use the [matchday graphic](../launch/assets/tiktok-matchday-cover.png) with `CLOCK IN. RUN ON.` and `DRAMATISATION` in off-white and a small `TMRW/.` lock-up.

| Time | Picture and sound | On-screen copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 3 s | An alarm reads 6:00. Cut from the buzz to a whistle in an empty tunnel. | `6 AM CASUAL SHIFT / MATCHDAY` |
| 3 to 10 s | Match cuts: lace work boots, lace footy boots; pull on a work glove, tape a wrist. | `SAME HANDS. DIFFERENT SHIFT.` |
| 10 to 18 s | A clock-in beep gives way to a wide shot of the actor running onto the field. | `YOU SEE KICK-OFF. NOT THE SHIFT BEFORE IT.` |
| 18 to 24 s | End card: cut to black with `TMRW/.` and `Line up your Future` above the final off-white line. Hold it long enough to read. | Nominate the athlete in your postcode |

**Music brief:** Tense, stripped-back instrumental that builds to the run-on, 105 to 115 BPM. Search `gritty drums`, `tension build` or `instrumental sports pulse` in TikTok’s in-app Commercial Music Library. Leave room for the alarm, clock-in beep and whistle.

**Caption:** Dramatisation. You see the game. You don’t always see the shift that came first. Follow TMRWLine and share this with the mate who goes straight from work to training. To nominate someone, send us a private message with your postcode. Ask the athlete before sharing their details.

Line up your Future

**Hashtags:** #TMRWLine #GrassrootsSport #Matchday

**@ tags:** None.

**Measure:** Full watches, shares and private messages about nominations.
