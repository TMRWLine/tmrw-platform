# TMRWLine launch campaigns | 19 November 2026

**Purpose:** Introduce TMRWLine and show who keeps local sport going, what happens before the game and where sponsorship money goes. These are drafts. The founder, cast and any club or creator must be cleared before use. Don't name an example athlete or a scouted prospect. If no adult is cleared, use the approved founder or anonymous fallback. Don't publish without approval.

**Visual direction:** Use one real action still and one bold headline on each vertical cover. Keep the headline in the centre crop and check it on a phone. Use the [cover guide](../launch/assets/phone-first-cover.svg), not the old diagram covers. Shoot high-contrast monochrome, plain clothing and blank props. No team logos, club emblems or trademarked jerseys. Hold the [end card](../launch/assets/end-card.png) long enough to read **Every postcode has a tomorrow** above the primary lockup. The lockup keeps `LINE UP YOUR FUTURE` in bold caps and its volt `#B8FF00` marks on dark.

**Friday Postcode Call-Out:** “Who keeps your local game going? Send us your postcode privately first.” Run it on TikTok and Instagram Stories. Don't ask for names in comments. See the [moderation notes](../launch/account-setup.md#moderation-before-launch).

## LinkedIn Company Page | The Local Receipt

**Format:** Five silent portrait slides. Label the sample and the actual survey evidence separately. The [receipt still](../launch/assets/linkedin-receipt.png) is a sample-only static alternative; include the source in the caption if using it. For later short-form adaptations, use a real photo of a blank receipt being marked up and one headline, **A$2,500 in. Where's it go?** Keep the sample label readable.

| Slide | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | `A$2,500 IN. WHO GETS WHAT?` and `ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE / NOT A PAYMENT` |
| 2 | `A$1,750 / ATHLETE (70%)` and `ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE / NOT A PAYMENT` |
| 3 | `A$375 / GRASSROOTS CLUB (15%)` and `ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE / NOT A PAYMENT` |
| 4 | `A$375 / TMRWLINE (15%)` and `ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE / NOT A PAYMENT` |
| 5 | `WHAT COULD THE CLUB SHARE COVER?` Add the sourced participation figure below with its survey group and date. Close with `EVERY POSTCODE HAS A TOMORROW` and the primary lockup. |

**Post copy:** In this worked example, A$1,750 supports the athlete, A$375 goes to the grassroots club and A$375 goes to TMRWLine to run the sponsorship. It's a sample, not a payment or a promise.

[Sport NZ's Active NZ 2024/25 survey](https://sportnz.org.nz/media/wfzflqoq/sport-nz-active-nz-report-24_25-final-v2.pdf#page=28) found that 35% of children aged 5 to 11 in Aotearoa New Zealand who wanted to do more physical activity said their family couldn't afford it, up 13 percentage points from 2023. That's a reported barrier, not an Australian fee quote or a measure of a funding gap. What would a club share help cover first at your ground: entry fees, gear or transport?

Every postcode has a tomorrow.

**Next receipt:** Use a dated fee schedule with the club's permission for a specific cost. Verify the currency, year, region and what the fee covers before quoting it.

**Music:** None for the document. For a video adaptation, prefer original paper and pen sounds; otherwise use a cleared instrumental at 75 to 85 BPM.

**Hashtags and @ tags:** #TMRWLine #SportsSponsorship #GrassrootsSport. None.

**Measure:** Shares and replies identifying a specific cost. For video adaptations, record three-second hold first.
