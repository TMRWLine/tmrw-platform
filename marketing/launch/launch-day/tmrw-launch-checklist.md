# TMRWLine | Launch checklist

**Every postcode has a tomorrow.** Use **TMRWLine** as the display name on Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn. The **tmrw/.** icon is the profile picture, not the account name.

## 1. Before you create anything

- [ ] Set aside a brand email inbox you control and a recovery phone. On LinkedIn, use your real personal profile to administer the Company Page; don't create a second personal profile for the brand.
- [ ] Put a unique password for each account in your password manager. Have an authenticator ready. Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) as each account is created and store recovery codes privately.
- [ ] Check **@tmrwline** on Instagram and TikTok, and **linkedin.com/company/tmrwline** on LinkedIn, before creating any account. If the first choice isn't available on all three, check **@tmrwlineanz** and **linkedin.com/company/tmrwlineanz** instead. Use the same available base on all three; stop and choose a matching alternative if neither works. None of these names is reserved or verified available.
- [ ] Keep this guide and the PNGs from `/home/ubuntu/launch-day/` together. Have your approved official website URL ready. If it isn't live or you don't know it yet, leave the website field blank where allowed. Don't paste placeholder text.

## 2. Create Instagram first

- [ ] Sign up, set the agreed handle and set **Name: TMRWLine**. Switch to a public **Professional** account and select **Business**. Pick **Sports & Recreation** if available, or the closest accurate sports category. Keep public contact buttons off unless a monitored brand inbox is ready.
- [ ] Upload **profile-picture.png**. Add your official site in **Edit profile > Links**. Turn on 2FA and paste this bio:
> Line up your Future  
> Local businesses backing athletes and grassroots clubs.  
> Follow for local sport and real stories.

## 3. Create TikTok second

- [ ] Create the account with the agreed username and set **Name: TMRWLine**. In account settings, switch to **Business Account** and choose the closest available sports or recreation category, such as **Sports & Outdoors** if offered. Keep the account public.
- [ ] Upload **profile-picture.png**, turn on 2FA, and add your official site in the website field if it's available. If not, leave it blank and add the link later. Paste this bio:
> Line up your Future  
> Local sport. Nominate an athlete in your postcode.

## 4. Create the LinkedIn Company Page third

- [ ] From your real personal profile, create a **Company Page**. Set **Name: TMRWLine**, the agreed Page URL and **Industry: Spectator Sports**. Complete company size and type truthfully. Use your official website URL; don't invent one if LinkedIn requires it.
- [ ] Upload **profile-picture.png** as the Page logo and **linkedin-banner.png** as the banner. Turn on 2FA for the admin's personal profile and give a trusted backup their own Page admin access rather than sharing your login.
- [ ] Paste this tagline:
> Line up your Future
- [ ] Paste this About:
> TMRWLine helps local businesses back athletes and grassroots clubs through sponsorship. We show how the money is split so businesses and clubs can see where their support goes. Follow for worked sponsorship examples, stories from local sport and ways to nominate an athlete in your postcode.

## 5. Connect the profiles and set up moderation

- [ ] Test all three public links on a phone. Add the profiles to the official site or link hub when ready. Use each app's available profile links for the other accounts where practical. Set the same brand picture everywhere.
- [ ] Instagram: allow Story replies and message requests; turn on Hidden Words and filter abusive terms. Set mentions and tags to a level you can moderate and use manual tag approval if available. After the first Story expires, save it to a **Postcodes** Highlight if the archive is on. Use **launch-ig-story.png** as the source for a cover or use the Story thumbnail; no separate Highlight cover is supplied.
- [ ] TikTok: use comment filtering and message requests where available. LinkedIn: enable Page messaging if someone is assigned to check it and keep comments on for discussion. App setting names and available controls can vary. If private messaging isn't available, direct people to a monitored private inbox, never to public comments.
- [ ] Assign a named primary and backup for replies, abusive messages and safeguarding. Check inboxes before posting. Never ask for names or contact details in public. Take nominations privately, postcode first; contact about juniors goes through a parent or guardian. Keep identifying details out of shared logs.

Keep team marks, trademarked jerseys and identifying details out of anonymous shots. Use original sound first; **library** means that app's commercial-use library only. ## 7. Handle the first hour after each post

- [ ] Open the live post on your phone. Check the crop, captions, sound and that the copy and hashtags are right. Share the Instagram feed post or Reel to your own Story if useful; don't duplicate an existing Story card.
- [ ] Reply to genuine questions, note useful prompts and check private messages. Don't ask for names in public. Hide identifying comments or abuse, and send privacy, threats or junior cases to the assigned safeguarding lead. Avoid screenshots of private messages.
- [ ] Log the post and hand off any unresolved messages. Aim to reply to genuine questions within one working day. No boosts, ad account access or spend without a separate go-ahead.

## 8. Review at the end of the week

- [ ] On Monday, record each post's platform, opening and series. For video, start with three-second hold or the nearest measure the app reports. Add completions, shares and saves per 1,000 views, repeat viewing where available, net follows and reply times.
- [ ] Count usable private nominations and serious sponsor enquiries once each. Keep identifying details out of the review and paid results separate from unpaid.
- [ ] Decide one thing to repeat, one opening to test and one cut to pause. Note a person and date for each. If you add cost context to the receipt, cite [Sport NZ Active NZ 2024/25, p. 28](https://sportnz.org.nz/media/wfzflqoq/sport-nz-active-nz-report-24_25-final-v2.pdf#page=28) as NZ survey evidence, not an Australian fee quote. Keep the split labelled as a sample, not a payment.

## 9. Work through the follow list in order

- [ ] Follow **10 to 20 accounts a day across the platforms during week one**, starting with lists 1 to 3 below. Continue in order on later days; don't catch up by mass following. Verify the official profile using its website or a blue tick. The team checked the listed Instagram and TikTok handles on 19 November 2026; recheck before following.
- [ ] Don't follow-for-follow, mass follow or mass unfollow. Leave a useful comment when you have something to say, not a generic emoji. Following is not permission to name, tag, repost, Collab or use anyone's material.
- [ ] Don't follow or message juniors or junior teams. Don't follow scouted prospects or example roster entries; let the scout use the private claim-and-consent route. Follow eligible prospects only after they claim their profile and agree. Follow the public adult accounts below to listen only, not to feature or contact them for content without a separate written agreement.
- [ ] Use benchmarks to study only: no tags, duets, stitches or reuse. Follow grassroots clubs through their official accounts, not players, once in talks about a cleared story. For wellbeing accounts, never reuse their material as ours or give mental health advice; have relevant crisis lines ready in moderation notes.
