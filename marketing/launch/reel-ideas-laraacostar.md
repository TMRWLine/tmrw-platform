# Ideas from the "educational storyteller" reel

**Reel:** [@laraacostar](https://www.instagram.com/reels/Df55_xKi3XX/), 114 seconds, about 3,000 views when checked on 19 November 2026. A LinkedIn creator talks to camera about how she writes posts. Her claims about dopamine and her viral numbers are hers, not checked, so don't quote them.

## Her framework

1. **Hook:** start with a real moment, the time you nearly quit or the time it finally worked.
2. **Leave the hook unfinished:** stop the first lines mid-sentence, so the "see more" cut-off does the work.
3. **Payoff:** answer the question the reader already has, as a short simple list.
4. **Familiar phrase, then your own line:** use a saying people already know, then follow it with something only you would say.
5. **PS:** end with a PS so the last line reads like a text from a friend.

## What we use

The Day 1 and Day 7 LinkedIn founder posts in the [launch pack](launch-pack.md) fit this best. Keep the pack's captions as the safe default; use the drafts below only if you can fill the brackets with something true. Don't invent a story or a quote.

**Day 1, 12:15 pm: Why start with a postcode?**

> The first time I tried to [your real moment: e.g. find a local sponsor for a young athlete], I nearly gave up.
>
> It wasn't the money that stopped it. It was
>
> [after "see more"] … [finish the sentence: what actually got in the way].
>
> So we're starting with one postcode, because:
> - [reason 1, e.g. a local business should know exactly who it is backing]
> - [reason 2, e.g. every dollar should be easy to follow]
> - [reason 3, e.g. the club down the road should share in it]
>
> They say it takes a village. Every postcode has a tomorrow.
>
> PS. If you run a local business, what would you need to know before backing local sport? I'll read every reply.

**Day 7, 12:15 pm: What would make a deal easier?**

> This week we put a sample A$2,500 split online. The question I didn't expect was
>
> [after "see more"] … [a real question someone asked this week].
>
> What we're hearing local businesses want before they sign:
> - [only things people actually said this week]
>
> [Familiar phrase], then [your own line].
>
> PS. What would you ask before backing an athlete and a club? Reply here or message the page.

The A$2,500 split is an illustrative sample, not a payment or a promise: say so in the post, as the Day 4 caption does.

## Also worth using

- Her on-screen format matches the first reel: a big title at the top with a small line under it in brackets, and captions one short phrase at a time. Use it for the founder videos.
- A soft CTA to "comment a word" can work, but only with replies Kane types himself. No auto-DMs, and no RSVP or event links until there is a real event.

## Leave out

- Words like "psychology", "make them fall for you" or "dopamine" in TMRWLine copy. They don't suit a brand that asks families for postcodes.
- Copying her posts, wording or visuals.
- Personal stories about athletes, juniors or clubs. A story is Kane's own, or it needs documented consent.
