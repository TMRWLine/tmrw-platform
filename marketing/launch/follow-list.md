# TMRWLine | Accounts to follow at launch

Who the new `tmrw/.` Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn accounts follow from day one. Use it with the [social media strategy](social-strategy.md) and [account setup](account-setup.md).

Handles were checked against each account's public Instagram or TikTok profile on 19 November 2026. Follower counts are rounded public figures from that day. Confirm the blue tick or official link before you follow, because look-alike accounts are common. Several obvious handles belong to someone else (see [Handles to avoid](#handles-to-avoid)).

## Rules

- **Following is not featuring.** Following an account does not give us permission to name, tag, repost, Collab or use anything of theirs. The consent and rights rules in the [strategy](social-strategy.md#evidence-rights-and-replies) still apply to every post.
- **Don't follow scouted prospects or the example roster.** People in the scout intake or `src/data/roster.ts` hear from us only through the claim-and-consent route. A follow from a brand account can feel like contact before they have opted in. Follow an athlete after they claim their profile and agree to it.
- **No junior accounts.** Don't follow or message junior athletes or junior teams. Contact for juniors goes through a parent or guardian only.
- **Benchmarks are for study.** Follow them to watch their craft, but never tag, duet, stitch or reuse their material.
- **Build up gradually.** Follow 10 to 20 accounts a day in the first week, starting with sections 1 to 3. Avoid follow-for-follow, mass following or unfollowing. Leave a useful comment when there's something to say rather than a generic emoji.
- **Clubs come later.** Follow each grassroots club when we start talking to it about a cleared story, and follow its official account, not a player's.

## Should the ANZ contender scout do this?

No. The scout finds individual prospects for private, consent-first intake and must keep them unpublished. This list is public brand accounts the social channels follow and learn from. Keep the two separate. When a scouted athlete claims their profile and agrees, they can be added to section 6.
