## First-week posting plan

Day 1 starts once the three accounts are ready. The dates below assume Friday, 20 November 2026; if launch moves, keep the order and use the indicated times as starting points, not guarantees. Times are Sydney time (AEDT). The three [19 November scripts](../daily-campaigns/2026-11-19.md) are slotted on days 1, 2 and 4.

| Day | AEDT | Platform and format | Hook | Caption | CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1, Fri 20 Nov | 6:30 pm | Instagram Reel: It Starts on Your Court | Every game starts somewhere. | First post. We’re TMRWLine. We’re here to help local businesses back athletes and grassroots clubs. Line up your Future | Follow and send it to the mate who gets the game going. |
| 2, Sat 21 Nov | 6:00 pm | TikTok: Clock In. Run On. | 6 AM casual shift / matchday | Dramatisation. You see the game. You don’t always see the shift that came first. Line up your Future | Nominate by private message with your postcode. Ask before sharing anyone’s details. |
| 3, Sun 22 Nov | 5:00 pm | Instagram Stories: two-frame poll | What keeps your local game going? | Think about your last game. What would make it easier for someone new to play? | Vote: help with entry fees or gear to borrow. |
| 4, Mon 23 Nov | 12:15 pm | LinkedIn document: The A$2,500 Receipt | A$2,500 in. Who gets what? | Sample, not a payment: A$1,750 to the athlete, A$375 to the grassroots club and A$375 to TMRWLine. Line up your Future | Share it with a local business and tell us how your club would use A$375. |
| 5, Tue 24 Nov | 6:30 pm | TikTok: hands-only nomination guide | Got a nomination? Start with your postcode. | Send your postcode privately and ask the athlete before you share their details. No names in the comments, please. | Save this or send it to someone who knows local sport. |
| 6, Wed 25 Nov | 7:00 pm | Instagram carousel: three original stills | The game starts before anyone cheers. | A ball, a painted line and the lights coming on. Here’s to the people who get local sport ready to play. | Share to your Story and tell us what gets your local game started. |
| 7, Thu 26 Nov | 12:15 pm | LinkedIn text post: question for local businesses | What would make local sponsorship easier to say yes to? | We showed a sample split this week. If you run a local business, what would you want to know before backing an athlete and a club? | Comment with the first question you would ask. |

Keep every launch post athlete-free. Use original imagery, leave location tags off, and do not tag benchmark accounts, athletes or clubs. Choose music in-app for Instagram and TikTok from each platform’s commercial-use library. Keep the LinkedIn document silent; use the [receipt still](assets/linkedin-receipt.png) as the static alternative.
