## Before launch

- Invite **adult players, coaches and volunteers** privately: “What keeps you coming back to local sport? We're making short local stories. Taking part is your choice. If you're interested, we'll explain where the footage would appear and what you can decline before we record.” Do not recruit through public nomination details or use the example or scouted roster. Log signed releases and media rights. Check clear footage, locations, music and rights before booking any post. For each intro, record the founder's own approval.
- Invite **2 to 3 clubs** to sign an agreement for a cleared story and Instagram Collab post. Line up **3 to 5 local creators** for potential paid commissions in week one. Use the [rights and fee checklist](social-strategy.md#clubs-and-creators-from-week-one). These are recruitment goals, not signed agreements. No fee, spend or ad account access without the user's separate go-ahead. Nothing here sends invitations or commits funds.
- Assign named people to the moderation shifts below and verify inbox access and handover. If the cast, collab or photo isn't cleared, substitute an approved founder cut or an anonymous cut. If the moderator or a safe publishable cut is unavailable, hold the slot.

## Moderation before launch

Assign a named primary and backup to each slot before the first post. Use the same roster across TikTok, Instagram, the Company Page and founder posts; the founder forwards issues without reposting private details. Pause new posts if nobody can cover a slot.

| Coverage | Primary role | Backup role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekdays, 9 am and 6 pm Sydney time | Social lead | Founder |
| Weekends, 10 am and 6 pm Sydney time | Duty moderator | Social lead |
| First hour after each post | Post owner | Duty moderator |
| Junior, privacy or abuse escalation | Named safeguarding lead | Named senior backup |

Check handovers at the end of each shift and answer good-faith messages within one working day. Keep only the necessary private record; log the time, action and assigned owner, not personal details in the shared notes.

- **Nomination:** “Thanks for thinking of someone. Send us your postcode privately first, with no names. Please ask the adult before sharing any details.”
- **Junior:** “Please don't share a young person's name, school or contact details here. A parent or guardian can contact us privately.” Hide identifying comments, stop direct contact with the junior and alert the safeguarding lead immediately.
- **Genuine criticism or incorrect figures:** “Thanks for flagging that. We'll check it and update this post if we got it wrong.” Check the source and correct visibly; don't delete good-faith criticism.
- **Abuse, threats or exposed details:** Hide or remove exposed details and abusive content, preserve only the minimum record securely and alert the safeguarding lead immediately. Block and report when warranted. For a credible immediate threat, follow the local emergency process. Don't debate the person or reuse the incident as content.
