# Ideas from the "build the queue" reel

**Reel:** [@thatbrojonny, Part 2](https://www.instagram.com/reels/Df4lksBI4_n/), 84 seconds, about 34,500 views when checked on 19 November 2026. A founder talks to camera about getting your first 10,000 customers for free. His two tips:

1. **Build the queue before the product.** He says Robinhood started with a one-box email page that showed your place in the queue, and you moved up for every friend you invited.
2. **Reward the share.** He says Harry's launch page gave free product for 5, 10 and 50 friend invites.

He ends with "win one small room, then the next" and "comment POSTED and I'll DM you my tool".

His numbers (10,000 sign-ups on day one, 100,000 emails in a week) are his claims. We haven't checked them, so don't quote them in our posts.

This review only covers social and marketing ideas. Anything that needs a waitlist page or a referral feature on the website belongs to the website bot.

## What we won't copy

- **"Comment POSTED for my auto-post tool"**: we publish nothing automatically, and we don't use bait-for-DM lead magnets.
- **Clickbait claims** like "10,000 customers with zero money": we make no growth or payout promises, and we only use numbers we've sourced.
- **Brand case studies with their logos** (Robinhood, Harry's screenshots): don't show other brands' marks or screenshots in our videos.
- **Referral prizes, or a leaderboard of named people**: no prizes without approval and a legal check, and never rank or name nominators, athletes or juniors.

## Handover to the website bot (not this Bot's work)

If Kane wants a real waitlist, the website bot could build a postcode waitlist page with a "you're #N in your postcode" confirmation and a share link once the domain is live. Socials would then point "link in bio" there. Until that exists, the link in bio goes to the plain site URL, and the Postcode Queue runs in DMs and Stories only.

## Suggested first test (week two, after launch week)

Make one TikTok in the reel's format: founder to camera with the question **"Which postcode should we start in?"**, word-by-word captions and a "comment POSTCODE" call to action, with replies sent by hand. Judge it on three-second hold and the number of usable postcodes, compared with the week-one founder intro.
