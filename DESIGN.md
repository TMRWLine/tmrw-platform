# DESIGN.md // tmrw/. Master Creative & Aesthetic Specification

## 1. Aesthetic Framework
- High-contrast, institutional clearinghouse aesthetic.
- Zero decorative padding, no soft shadows, no rounded consumer cards.
- Dark canvas foundation paired with monospace coordinate telemetry.

## 2. Master Chromatics
- Monolithic Carbon Black: #080808 (Canvas background).
- Signal White: #F9F9F8 (High-contrast text and ledger surfaces).
- Kinetic Volt: #D2FF00 (Active telemetry accents, radar nodes, active buttons only).
- Hyper Electric Cobalt: #0038FF (Active ledger verification, pre-cleared escrow locks).
- Structural Hairline Dark: #1A1A1A (Borders, radar grid rings).
- Structural Hairline Light: #E5E5EA (Light-mode dividers).

## 3. Typographic Governance
- Master Mark: Neue Haas Grotesk Display Bold (all-lowercase: tmrw/.).
- Descriptors: HTF Tungsten Medium (all-caps, tight tracking).
- Display Headings: Inter Display (weight 800, tracking -0.03em).
- Body Copy: Inter Regular/Medium (weight 400/500, New Zealand English spelling).
- Telemetry & Ledger Data: JetBrains Mono (weight 400/500, uppercase stamps).

## 4. The 23.0° Dynamic Framing Beam
- Every action card, photography crop, and layout divider anchors to a 23.0° kinetic forward rake.
- Alternate between Monolithic Carbon Black surfaces and stark Signal White ledgers to establish visual tension.

## 5. Micro-Mark System
- For containers at or below 32x32px, drop the wordmark tmrw and isolate the slash and dot (/.).
